# GIG ECONOMY

  **Platform & Gig Economy**

  > _"The **gig economy** is the economic system by which a workforce of people (known as gig workers) engage in freelance and/or side-employment."_
  >
  > [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gig_economy)

  > _"A gig economy is characterized by temporary, contract, and freelance jobs rather than permanent positions. Gig workers enjoy flexibility but often lack job security and essential benefits such as health insurance. Employers benefit by saving costs and hiring from a larger, globally accessible talent pool. Potential downsides include disrupted work-life balance, financial insecurity for workers, and limited career development."_
  > 
  > [Source: Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/g/gig-economy.asp)

  > _"The **platform economy** encompasses economic and social activities facilitated by digital platforms such as Amazon, Airbnb, Uber, Microsoft and Google."_
  >
  > [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_economy)
